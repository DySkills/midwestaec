# Barndominium & Post-Frame Thermal Envelopes: Zone 5 Condensation Control

> **Executive Summary:** Post-frame construction ("barndominiums") has surged across the rural Midwest due to rapid structural erection and clear-span interiors. However, residential occupancies generate massive internal moisture loads that commercial pole barns never face. Without an engineered thermal break and continuous outboard drainage plane, exterior steel panels become sub-zero condensation surfaces in Climate Zones 4 and 5, rotting structural timber columns within 7 to 10 years.

## 1. The Post-Frame Residential Moisture Trap
*Analyze the physical failure mode: 26-gauge corrugated steel siding fastened directly over wood girts creates thousands of conductive cold fingers. When warm 70°F indoor air at 40% RH contacts 0°F steel, winter condensation generates gallons of trapped liquid water behind batt insulation.*

## 2. Wall Assembly Comparison for Rural Climate Zone 5

| Layer | Traditional Pole Barn (Failure-Prone) | Engineered Residential Post-Frame |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Exterior Cladding** | 26-gauge steel screwed to wood girts | 26-gauge steel over 1x4 vertical furring |
| **Drainage Cavity** | None (direct contact) | 3/4" ventilated air space |
| **Continuous Layer** | Single-bubble foil | R-7.5 rigid continuous exterior insulation |
| **Structural Frame** | Solid sawn 6x6 posts | Glue-laminated columns on concrete perma-columns |
| **Cavity Insulation** | R-19 low-density fiberglass | Friction-fit mineral wool or open-cell spray foam |
| **Vapor Retarder** | 6-mil polyethylene | Class II variable-permeance polyamide membrane |

## 3. Commercial Girt Assemblies & Thermal Bridging
*Detailing exterior horizontal books-girts vs flush interior framing. How outboard continuous insulation breaks conduction paths from the steel skin into structural posts.*

## 4. Foundation & Ground Interface: Preventing Column Rot
1. **Concrete Wet-Set Brackets:** Keeping wood columns entirely out of ground contact using elevated pier footings.
2. **Sub-Slab Hydronic Integration:** R-10 continuous EPS beneath rural heated shop/living slabs with 15-mil poly vapor barrier.
3. **Rodent & Pest Proofing:** Heavy-gauge aluminum z-flashing and stainless steel mesh at the bottom corrugated edge.

## 5. Payback & Operating Economy
*Lifecycle analysis showing that spending 8% to 12% more on outboard continuous insulation and rainscreen strapping reduces rural propane and electric heating bills by over 45% annually.*
