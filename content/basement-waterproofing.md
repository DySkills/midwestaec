# Why Water Enters Midwest Basements After Heavy Rain (And Why 6-Mil Poly Fails)

> **Executive Summary:** The Midwest is dominated by heavy clay soils that expand when wet and contract during summer droughts. After heavy spring rains or rapid snowmelt, water saturates the clay surrounding your foundation, creating thousands of pounds of hydrostatic pressure. If water doesn't enter as bulk liquid through wall cracks, it migrates straight up through the porous concrete slab as invisible moisture vapor, destroying expensive luxury vinyl plank (LVP) and hardwood floors.

## 1. Hydrostatic Pressure & The "Clay Bowl" Effect
*Explain why newly dug foundation backfill settles more loosely than native virgin soil. During rain, this loose soil acts as a funnel, pooling water directly against foundation walls. Hydrostatic pressure forces water through cold joints (the seam where the poured floor meets the concrete wall).*

## 2. Why Cheap 6-Mil Construction Polyethylene Fails
*Explain what happens under the slab during concrete placement. Cheap 6-mil poly is made from recycled plastics that break down over 5 to 10 years in alkaline concrete environments. Furthermore, workers walking on rebar and gravel before the pour puncture cheap 6-mil poly hundreds of times, destroying its vapor barrier integrity.*

## 3. Specification Standards: Under-Slab Vapor Retarders

| Vapor Retarder Class | Puncture Resistance (ASTM D1709) | Tensile Strength (ASTM D882) | Permeance Rating (ASTM E96) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard 6-Mil Recycled Poly** | < 300 grams (Punctures easily) | < 30 lbs/inch | 0.06 – 0.08 Perms (When intact) |
| **ASTM E1745 Class C (10-Mil)** | ≥ 475 grams | ≥ 45 lbs/inch | ≤ 0.10 Perms |
| **ASTM E1745 Class B (12-Mil)** | ≥ 1,500 grams | ≥ 45 lbs/inch | ≤ 0.10 Perms |
| **ASTM E1745 Class A (Stego Wrap 15-Mil)** | **≥ 2,200 grams (Walk on it)** | **≥ 45 lbs/inch** | **0.0086 Perms (True Barrier)** |

## 4. Bulk Water vs. Moisture Vapor: The Dual Defense
1. **Interior Perimeter Drain (French Drain):** Relieves hydrostatic head pressure under the slab and directs water into a dual-pump sump pit with battery backup.
2. **True Class A Vapor Retarder:** Lapped 6 inches and taped with acrylic seam tape, sealed continuously to foundation stem walls to block 100% of subterranean water vapor.

## 5. Contractor Checklist for Finishing a Midwest Basement
*Checklist: Perform a 24-hour calcium chloride or plastic sheet moisture test on the bare slab before laying any flooring. If relative humidity under the plastic exceeds 75%, install a dimpled subfloor drainage mat (e.g., Dorken Delta-FL) before laying finished floors.*
