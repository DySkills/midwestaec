# How to Pass the Blower Door Test (< 3.0 ACH50) on a Midwest Flip or Remodel

> **Executive Summary:** The International Energy Conservation Code (IECC 2021/2024) mandates that new residential builds and gut renovations achieve an airtightness threshold of 3.0 ACH50 (air changes per hour at 50 Pascals of depressurization) in Climate Zones 4 and 5. For flippers and builders working on tight deadlines, failing a municipal blower door test delays occupancy permits and costs thousands in remedial troubleshooting. Passing the test is not about expensive materials—it is about following a strict 5-point sealing sequence prior to drywall.

## 1. What is 3.0 ACH50 and How is It Tested?
*Explain how the blower door test works: A calibrated fan is mounted in an exterior doorway, depressurizing the home to -50 Pascals relative to outside. The fan measures the cubic feet per minute (CFM50) required to maintain that pressure, calculating how many times the total volume of air in the house leaks out per hour.*

## 2. The 5 Leak Points That Cause 80% of Test Failures
1. **The Rim Joist & Sill Plate Connection:** The framing seam between the foundation concrete, mudsill, and rim joist.
2. **Top Plate Plumbing & Electrical Wire Penetrations:** Unsealed holes drilled through 2x4 top plates into unconditioned attic spaces.
3. **HVAC Duct Register Boots:** Gaps between sheet metal ceiling boots and drywall.
4. **Bathtubs & Showers on Exterior Walls:** Dropped subfloors under tubs that open directly to uninsulated floor cavities.
5. **Attic Access Hatches & Pull-Down Stairs:** Unweatherstripped plywood hatches acting as open air vents.

## 3. Specification Checklist: Sealing Schedule Prior to Insulation

| Inspection Area | Code Requirement | Recommended Sealing Method | Cost Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rim Joists** | IECC Section R402.4.1.1 | 2" Closed-Cell Spray Foam or Foil-Faced Polyiso + Foam Edge Seal | Low ($1.50/sq.ft) |
| **Top Plate Wire Holes** | ASTM E283 Airtightness | 1-Part Fire-Rated Polyurethane Foam | Minimal ($15/can) |
| **HVAC Ceiling Boots** | SMACNA Duct Leakage Standard | Mastic Tape or Water-Based Duct Mastic to Drywall | Minimal ($25/tub) |
| **Electrical Rough-in Boxes** | IECC R402.4.1.1 | Vapor-barrier gasket boxes or acoustical sealant caulk behind box | $1.50 / box |
| **Attic Hatch** | Continuous Weatherstrip | 3" Rigid Foam glued to back of hatch + closed-cell foam tape | $35 total |

*Checklist: Schedule a pre-drywall rough blower door test. Finding and sealing leaks at the rough stage costs $100; finding leaks after drywall and paint costs $2,500.*
