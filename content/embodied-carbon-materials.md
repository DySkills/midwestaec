# Embodied Carbon & Bio-Based Insulation: Decarbonizing Midwest Envelopes

> **Executive Summary:** While operational energy efficiency reduces a building's utility consumption over decades, upfront embodied carbon accounts for up to 50% of a high-performance building's lifetime climate footprint. In Climate Zones 4 and 5, choosing bio-based insulations (dense-pack cellulose, wood fiber) or basalt mineral wool instead of petroleum-derived extruded polystyrene (XPS) with high-GWP blowing agents eliminates thousands of kilograms of atmospheric greenhouse emissions before move-in.

## 1. Upfront Embodied Carbon vs. Operational Payback
*Break down the carbon curve: A thick layer of XPS insulation blown with HFC-134a can take 20 to 30 years of operational heating energy savings just to offset the chemical manufacturing emissions of the foam itself.*

## 2. Material Embodied Carbon & Global Warming Potential (GWP)

| Insulation Material | Global Warming Potential (kg CO₂e / m² at R-20) | Carbon Sequestered? | Primary Feedstock |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dense-Pack Cellulose** | -1.2 to +0.8 (Net Carbon Sink) | Yes (Wood fiber) | 85% Post-consumer recycled paper |
| **Rigid Wood Fiber Board** | -2.1 to -0.5 (Net Carbon Negative) | Yes (Timber byproducts) | Sustainable forest thinnings |
| **Stone Mineral Wool** | +4.5 to +7.2 | No (Mineral) | Natural basalt rock & slag |
| **Expanded Polystyrene (EPS)** | +8.5 to +12.0 | No (Petroleum) | Polymerized styrene / pentane |
| **Extruded Polystyrene (XPS)** | +35.0 to +85.0 | No (Petroleum) | Styrene with halogenated blowing agents |

## 3. Vapor Permeability & Wall Drying Mechanics
*Why bio-based insulations excel in Midwest mixed-humid/cold climates: Unlike non-permeable petrochemical foams that trap incidental wall leaks, wood fiber and dense-pack cellulose allow omnidirectional inward and outward drying, protecting wood framing from fungal decay.*

## 4. Fire Resistance & Thermal Performance
1. **Stone Wool:** Zero-flame spread, 2,150°F melt threshold, completely non-combustible.
2. **Borate-Treated Cellulose:** Class A fire rating, dense packing starves oxygen and blocks flame propagation.
3. **Wood Fiber Outboard:** Self-charring surface protective layer under ASTM E119 exposure.

## 5. Regional Sourcing Across the Midwest Basin
*Mapping Midwest manufacturing hubs for cellulose in Wisconsin, Indiana, and Ohio, reducing transportation emissions and supporting local circular agricultural and paper economies.*
