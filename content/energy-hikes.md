# Why Your Midwest Electric & Gas Bill Doubled (And the 2 Fixes That Actually Cut It)

> **Executive Summary:** With Midwest utility rate hikes hitting 30% to 50% across Ameren, ComEd, and Duke Energy, homeowners are looking for immediate relief. While window replacement companies spend millions advertising 40% energy savings, building science shows that windows account for less than 12% of winter envelope heat loss. The two highest-ROI retrofits that pay back in under 3 winters are ceiling plane air-sealing and dense-pack attic insulation.

## 2. The Stack Effect: How Your Attic Siphons Heat
*Explain the physics of the "Stack Effect" in winter. Warm air is buoyant and rises. It forces its way through hundreds of unsealed penetrations (can lights, electrical wires, plumbing vent stacks, attic hatch, chimney chases) into the cold attic. As warm air escapes the top, it draws frigid outdoor air in through basement rim joists and sill plates.*

## 4. The 2 Upgrades with Immediate Payback
1. **Ceiling Plane Air Sealing:** Moving existing insulation aside, sealing every drywall-to-framing penetration with 1-part expanding foam and fire-rated mastic before blowing in new insulation.
