High Risk in Zone 5

Standard 6-Mil Poly

Recycled Polyethylene Sheeting

ASTM E1745: Does Not Comply
Puncture Resistance: < 300 grams (Punctures on gravel)
Perm Rating: 0.06 Perms (when intact)
Degradation: Decomposes in 5-10 yrs under concrete
Field Reality: Workers walking with rebar puncture 6-mil poly hundreds of times before the pour. Moisture migrates directly into flooring, warping engineered hardwood.
MidwestAEC Recommended Spec

Stego Wrap 15-Mil

Prime Virgin Polyolefin (Class A)

ASTM E1745 Class A
Puncture Resistance: 2,200+ grams (Walk on it)
Water Permeance: 0.0086 Perms (True barrier)
Life Expectancy: Life of the structure
Radon Gas Blocker: Yes (Certified)
Why it pays: \$0.45/sq.ft installed vs \$0.15 for cheap 6-mil. On a 2,000 sq.ft basement slab, the difference is only \$600—preventing \$15,000 in ruined luxury vinyl tile.

20-Mil Reinforced Poly

Tear Resistance: Reinforced nylon scrim

Legacy Standard

Fiberglass Batts (R-13/15)

Air-permeable glass fibers

  • Real-World R: Drops ~30% with air leaks
  • Moisture Resistance: Loses R when damp
  • Fire Rating: Melts at ~1,000°F
Midwest Winner: Cavity

Rockwool Comfortbatt (R-15/23)

Dense stone mineral wool

  • Real-World R: R-4.2 per inch (True retention)
  • Hydrophobic: Repels liquid water entirely
  • Fire Rating: Withstands 2,150°F (Non-combustible)
Exterior Continuous

Continuous Polyiso / GPS (R-6 to R-13)

Outboard continuous envelope

  • Thermal Bridging: Stops 100% stud heat loss
  • Dew Point Control: Keeps sheathing warm
  • Zone 5 Code: ≥ R-5 continuous required

Old-School Thinking

Single-Stage AC + 80% Gas Furnace

Blasts 100% full capacity, cooling the house in 7 minutes. It shuts off before pulling latent moisture from the air, resulting in 65% indoor humidity and cold, clammy Midwest summers.

Modern Building Science

Inverter Cold-Climate Heat Pump + Dedicated Dehumidifier

Variable-speed compressor throttles down to 25% capacity, running long low-stage cycles to continuously wring humidity out of the air. In winter, vapor-injection compressors maintain 100% heating down to -5°F without auxiliary strip heat.
